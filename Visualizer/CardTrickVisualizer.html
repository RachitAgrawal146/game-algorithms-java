How It Works
21 cards are dealt into 3 columns of 7. Each round, you tell the algorithm which column contains your card. The chosen column is placed in the middle of the three stacks when gathering them back together. After 3 rounds, your card is guaranteed to be at position 11 (the centre) — a consequence of the mathematical structure of the rearrangement, not luck.
